# FILE #002 — Gravity visual treatment

Version: 0.1 — proposal for owner approval.

## Visual rule

For the first eight seconds, the absence of gravity is legible through small,
ordinary objects drifting upward in a controlled way. At the return, every
object settles safely. Only one coffee droplet continues upward afterward.

## Direction

- 9:16 cinematic realism with daylight contrast strong enough for mobile.
- A generic bus stop is the anchor location. It must have no transit logo,
  city name, timetable, route number or advertising brand.
- The same anonymous adult woman appears only within scenes 01, 03, 04 and 05.
  She wears a neutral dark jacket and carries a plain takeaway cup with no logo.
- Keep motion slow, readable and physically consistent within each beat: no
  morphing hands, duplicated cups, unstable faces or objects changing identity.
- Use sky, pavement and the bus-stop post as stable reference lines so the
  vertical movement reads immediately.
- Avoid wide disaster imagery, real news aesthetics, emergency colour systems,
  sirens, panic, impact shots and injuries.

## Sound and text

- Begin with a restrained low-frequency lift and a short vacuum-like hush;
  never use an emergency tone or mobile operating-system sound.
- At the return, use one soft low impact followed by near silence for the
  droplet reveal.
- `8 SECONDS.` and `FILE #002 — ARCHIVED` are editorial overlays, not in-world
  messages. They use the archive style from the Visual Bible: centered,
  letter-spaced sans text with thin rules and no red cursor.
- No in-world screen, notification or message interface appears in this FILE.

## Approval needed before assets

The owner must approve this visual treatment alongside the final script and
storyboard before keyframes, video clips, narration or sound are generated.
