## Owner actions
These steps require the channel owner or account access and cannot be completed solely from the repository.

- [ ] Confirm final channel name after handle/search availability check.
- [ ] Create or rename the YouTube channel.
- [ ] Reserve the best available YouTube handle.
- [ ] Confirm repository visibility should be Private if proprietary strategy/assets will live here.
- [ ] Approve avatar/logo direction.
- [ ] Approve banner direction.
- [ ] Confirm preferred production tool stack for video generation/editing/voice.
- [ ] Upload and publish the first video when production is approved.
