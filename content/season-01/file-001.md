# FILE #001 — Everyone Received the Same Message at 3:17 AM

Status: development
Version: 0.1

## Core hook
At exactly 3:17 AM, every phone on Earth receives the same message:

`DON'T LOOK AT THE MOON.`

## Goal
Test universal mystery, immediate stakes, simple English, strong visual hook and a final reveal that can seed the wider lore.

## Short target
25–35 seconds.

## Draft beat sheet
### 0–2s — Cold open
Millions of phones light up at the same time.
On-screen text / narration:
`At 3:17 AM, every phone on Earth received the same message.`

### 2–6s — Message
Close-up:
`DON'T LOOK AT THE MOON.`

### 6–12s — Human reaction
People laugh, record videos, open curtains and point cameras at the sky.

### 12–20s — Escalation
The Moon looks normal at first.
Then every live stream glitches simultaneously.

### 20–27s — Reveal
When the signal returns, the Moon is no longer in the same position.
It has moved impossibly close.

### 27–33s — Sting
A second message arrives:
`FILE #001 HAS BEGUN.`

Optional final visual: timestamp 03:17 appears again.

## Narrative rules
- Do not imply this is real news.
- Keep geography generic/global.
- No identifiable real government or agency branding.
- The Moon must remain visually recognizable.
- The final message is a lore experiment; remove if testing shows it feels too explanatory.

## Visual requirements
1. Phone screens across different environments.
2. Clear readable message.
3. Window/sky shot with Moon.
4. Global livestream montage without using copyrighted broadcast footage.
5. Impossible Moon movement.
6. Final phone notification.

## Sound direction
- Immediate notification cascade.
- Low atmospheric tension.
- Brief silence before Moon reveal.
- Single deep impact at reveal.

## Metrics to evaluate
- Viewed vs swiped away.
- Retention at 2s, 5s and 15s.
- Average percentage viewed.
- Replays.
- Comments mentioning theories/connections.
- Shares.

## Expansion paths if winner
- FILE #006 — The Moon Moved.
- Long-form: `What Happened at 3:17 AM?`
- Follow-up from the perspective of The Archivist.
