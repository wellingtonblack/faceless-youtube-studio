# FILE #002 — Gravity Disappeared for 8 Seconds

Version: 0.1 — draft for owner approval. Lifecycle status lives only in

## Core premise

For eight seconds, gravity vanishes everywhere. When it returns, one drop of
coffee continues to rise into the sky.

## Creative brief

- Runtime: 28–32 seconds; vertical 9:16.
- English: simple, international and tense; neutral narration.
- Hook: loss of gravity must be immediately legible without captions.
- Characters: anonymous people only; no recurring character or Archivist.
- The final rising droplet is a self-contained visual mystery, not an approved
  connection to another FILE.
- Fictional cinematic treatment only: no simulated breaking news, emergency
  alert, real city, logo, broadcaster, agency or platform interface.
- Do not show injuries, collisions, children in danger, vehicle crashes or
  realistic mass panic.
